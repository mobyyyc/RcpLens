## Ranked experiments for later approval

1. **Receipt row/column association:** compare current grouping with installed Vision document structure and conservative price-column association, preserving raw observations and evidence coordinates. First inspect the six association suspects across all three stores, then score the same images and new held-out purchases. This is the strongest cross-store lead for P2-02; it is not proof that document recognition will improve them.
2. **Retailer interpretation and field boundaries:** retain SKU/printed-description evidence while testing wrapped rows, negative discounts, item-section termination, canonical merchant extraction, printed dates and totals. The three Costco evidence-present deficits and two No Frills merchant-header mismatches make this a concrete P2-03 lead. A correct printed total must never excuse missed purchases.
3. **Capture/OCR quality and candidate retention:** privately adjudicate the three unresolved missing lines and obtain problematic phone photos before choosing crop, perspective, quality checks, alternate text candidates or language-correction experiments. Preserve long-receipt coverage and original coordinates; report per-store regressions. The current audit cannot choose an image treatment from measured photo-quality evidence.
4. **Correction workflow timing:** time source-assisted review/edit/save on the user's phone, then prioritize the operations actually taking time in P2-04. The count of 20 is a proxy, not a finding that the user can finish in any particular number of seconds.

These are proposals, not authorization to run another milestone. No Foundation Models/cloud/Apple Intelligence path was run or introduced. The user's China-market iPhone 15 Pro remains the required personal device; this baseline creates no Apple Intelligence dependency.
